# About us

IPE is an independent think tank, focused on economic policy. We are located in The Hague, The Netherlands. We are part of the [European Macro Policy Network (EMPN)](https://empn.eu/), a decentralized network of European think tanks. If you would like to learn more about us, please [contact us](/contact/).
