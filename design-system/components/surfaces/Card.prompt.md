White content surface — hairline gray border, small radius, minimal shadow. Brand identity from an optional red top-rule, not chrome.

```jsx
<Card topRule interactive>
  <MonoLabel bullet>Rapport</MonoLabel>
  <h3>Eerlijker en eenvoudiger armoedebeleid</h3>
</Card>
```

`topRule` adds a 3px brand rule (set `ruleColor` to use an accent). `interactive` lifts the card on hover. `padding` controls inner spacing.
